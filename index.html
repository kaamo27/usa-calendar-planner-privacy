Privacy Policy

How the app handles your information.

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the USA Calendar & Planner Android application (the “App”) handles information. This policy describes the development version reviewed for this page. If the App's features or third-party services change, this policy will be updated.

At a glance: The reviewed source stores calendar events and tasks on your device. It does not contain an advertising SDK, account registration, or a permission to access your precise location.

1. Information you enter

You may enter information such as event or task titles, descriptions, locations, dates, times, categories, and reminder preferences. In the reviewed version, this information is saved in a database on your device and is used to display your calendar and schedule reminders.

2. Where your information is stored

Calendar events and tasks are stored locally on your device. The reviewed version does not provide an account-based cloud sync or a developer-operated server for storing this personal calendar information. Android device backup settings may affect app data backup; you can review your device's backup settings.

3. Notifications and reminders

If you enable reminders and grant notification permission where Android requires it, the App can schedule local notifications for your events and tasks. The reviewed source also includes system components to restore scheduled reminders after the device restarts. Notification content is generated from the information you entered.

4. Location information

The reviewed Android manifest does not request precise or approximate device-location permission. An event may include a location that you type yourself; that text is stored with the event on your device.

5. Third-party services and internet access

The reviewed source contains code intended to retrieve supplemental US holiday information from the Nager.Date holiday API and cache the response on the device. However, the reviewed Android manifest does not declare the Android Internet permission, so this network feature may not function in this exact build. If a future release enables this connection, the App will send the requested holiday year and country code to that service. Please review the service provider's privacy information before using a release that enables this feature.

The reviewed source does not include Google AdMob, Google Analytics, Firebase, or another advertising or analytics SDK. If such services are added in a future release, this policy will be updated to explain the data they collect and the available consent choices before that release is distributed.

6. Data sharing

In the reviewed version, the developer does not receive or sell the event and task details you enter. The App does not include a feature for sharing your personal calendar data with the developer. You may independently share information using Android or other apps outside the App.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your locally stored events and tasks remain on your device until you delete them within the App (where supported), clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling or clearing storage may permanently remove locally stored information. Keep your own copy of anything important.

8. Children's privacy

The reviewed version is a general-purpose calendar and planner and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

The App uses Android's app-private local storage for its database. No method of electronic storage is completely secure. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep Android up to date.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when App features, permissions, or third-party services change. The latest version will be published at this page with a revised effective date.
