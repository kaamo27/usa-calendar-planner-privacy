This Privacy Policy explains how USA Calendar Planner ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about data collection, storage, advertising and third-party services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the App may process the following information:

  • Calendar events, personal reminders and tasks entered by you.
  • Preferences such as selected calendar settings and display options.
  • Advertising-related information processed by Google AdMob, where applicable.
  • Consent and privacy preference information.
  • Technical information required for app operation and troubleshooting.

We do not ask users to provide sensitive personal information to use the basic calendar features.

2. Calendar Events and Personal Data Storage

Events, tasks and reminders that you create may be stored locally on your device using the App's local database.

Unless a feature explicitly states otherwise, locally stored calendar information is not automatically uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the App or clearing its data may permanently remove locally stored information.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process certain information to provide, deliver, measure and improve advertisements.

Depending on applicable laws and your consent choices, this information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers, where available.
  • Device information and app interaction data.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Ad impressions, clicks and advertising performance information.
  • Information used for personalized or non-personalized advertising.

The availability and type of advertisements may depend on your location, app configuration and consent preferences.

For more information, please review Google's Advertising Privacy Policy .

4. Google UMP Consent and Privacy Choices

The App may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where required.

Depending on your region and applicable privacy requirements, you may be presented with choices regarding personalized advertising, non-personalized advertising and data processing.

Where required and supported by the App, you can revisit or change your consent preferences through the "Privacy Choices" option in the App settings.

Changing your consent preferences may affect the advertisements that are available to you.

More information about Google's consent tools: Google AdMob Privacy & Messaging .

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to support advertising, calendar information or app functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: Google Terms and Policies

If an external holiday data source or API is enabled in the App, requests to that service may expose technical information such as IP address and request details to the service provider.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal calendar events, tasks or reminders.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers, including Google, where necessary for advertising, consent management, security, analytics (if enabled) or app functionality.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Calendar events, tasks and reminders stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Advertising-related data handled by third-party providers is subject to their retention policies and applicable laws.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of certain personal information. You may also be able to manage advertising consent through the Privacy Choices feature, where available.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, third-party services or legal requirements. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
